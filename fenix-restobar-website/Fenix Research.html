1 · How Tashkent actually chooses a restaurant

Maps first

Discovery starts on Yandex Maps (ratings, photos, hours, phone) and Tripadvisor for visitors. Name, address and phone on the site must match those listings exactly or search ranking suffers.

Yandex Maps · Tashkent restaurants · Tripadvisor

Telegram is the CRM

Telegram has 24M+ users in Uzbekistan, more than Instagram (vendor figure). Uzum Tezkor's own research says over a third of Tashkent residents have ordered food from a restaurant directly through Telegram.

Zoomda · AliPos

Phone still closes the deal

Even top venues (Caravan, the places Afisha lists) take bookings by phone. Every Fenix post ends with two phone numbers. The site should make calling one tap, not swap it for a form.

Afisha.uz · Caravan

Delivery = two platforms

Yandex Eats and Uzum Tezkor dominate. Wolt left on 5 March 2026 and Uzum Tezkor took on its couriers. Commission runs 15–35% per order.

Kun.uz · Delever

New: couriers without the marketplace

Since 14 Sept 2026 Uzum Tezkor delivers orders a restaurant takes on its own channels (site, Telegram, phone). Fenix keeps its menu, prices and customer, and the platform only supplies the courier. That makes Phase 2 ordering realistic.

AliPos, Sept 2026

Booking apps exist, but are thin

Arbuz (arb.uz) offers 360° tours, menus and booking. Yandex booking requires a Yandex ID. Neither replaces a direct channel for banquets, where most of the money is.

arb.uz · UzNews

3 · What the world's best restaurant sites do

Noma · Eleven Madison Park

Editorial restraint. The interface gets out of the way, and photography and a strict palette carry the brand. Noma shows live Copenhagen weather in the header, a small detail that places it in its city.

Takea quiet frame, little chrome, one accent colour

Dishoom

Builds a world around the venue: every location has its own story, and the menu reads like print, not a list of cards.

Takea printed-menu feel and a strong story for the room

Momofuku

Sends each guest to the right booking channel for that location and that need. Its story paragraphs have small photos set inside the sentences.

Takea different path for each job: table, banquet, delivery

Chipotle · Sweetgreen

The ordering flow is the product. Repeat orders get the top spot, and there's a sticky mobile button and a live status tracker.

Takea bottom action bar on phones and a status screen for Phase 2

Across sources, the pattern is the same: the mobile site is the real homepage, the reservation step must stay visible, larger parties need their own path, and speed builds trust. Toast reports 86% of diners check a restaurant's website before a first visit.

4 · Recommendations for Fenix

  1. Make the site work like an app on phones. Add a fixed bottom bar with Call · Menu · Reserve. Call is first because guests here call first. The hero shows open/closed status, computed on Tashkent time.
  2. Sell the halls, not just dinner. Two halls for 120 and 170 guests, with weddings and corporate events, are what set Fenix apart. Events get their own page and form, and parties of 13+ are sent there from the booking form. A Tweak lets the owner try an "events-first" homepage (brief §13.1).
  3. Put the menu in HTML. The owner's PDF and the Telegram bar card become 211 searchable items in three tabs (Europe / Korea / Bar), with sticky category chips and deep links that also work as QR codes on the tables.
  4. Use real photos, not AI images. The concept's AI renders show a room that doesn't exist. The prototype uses stills from Fenix's own Telegram video: the real timber, red chairs, bar, stage and DJ. Book a proper photo shoot before launch.
  5. Make Telegram the back end. Booking and event requests go to a staff Telegram chat (cheapest, and staff already use it). The success screen says "this is a request, not a confirmation".
  6. Phase 2: own ordering, aggregator couriers. Cart → Payme / Click / Uzum / cash → status tracker, with delivery through Uzum Tezkor's new courier service. Every order needs a fiscal receipt. Card-to-card transfers without a receipt break tax law.
  7. Keep it fast. No framework: about 40 KB of JS plus data, WebP stills of 20–60 KB each, self-hosted subset fonts, lazy-loaded images, and a pre-loaded hero.

5 · What's in the prototype

Pages

Home · Menu (Europe / Korea / Bar) · Events · Gallery with keyboard lightbox · Contact · Reserve · Privacy. Russian is the default; Uzbek and English switch live.

Tweaks

Hero (triptych / single / type only) · wordmark (real logo / serif) · homepage order (dining / events first) · accent colour · Phase 2 ordering on/off · owner markers · frame · grain.

Accessibility

Skip link, visible focus ring, real labels, text error messages with aria-describedby, 44px tap targets, focus-trapped drawer, lightbox and order sheet, Esc to close, reduced-motion support, no zoom lock. The "faint" text colour was raised to pass contrast.

Data sources

Menu from menu_text.txt (189 items) plus the Telegram bar card (coffee, 10 lemonade flavours, snacks). Facts are marked verified, draft or owner-to-confirm.

6 · Still needed from the owner

Each of these shows as a ◇ marker in the prototype. You can hide the markers with Tweaks.

  • Official name: "FENIX Restobar" or "Fenix Restaurant & Bar"
  • Hours confirmed, last seating, holiday closures
  • Hall capacity confirmed (Telegram says 120 / 170; concept says 100)
  • Reply time promised on the success screen
  • Event packages, minimum guests, deposit
  • Yandex Maps and Google Business organisation links
  • DJ / live music schedule
  • Six signature dishes (current picks are mine)
  • Price confirmation for all 211 items
  • Who receives requests: Telegram chat, email, or both
  • Legal entity name and privacy policy text
  • Where personal data is stored (§7.7 — needs a lawyer)
  • Delivery: own couriers or Uzum Tezkor, zones, minimum order
  • Original photos and video, or a photo shoot date
